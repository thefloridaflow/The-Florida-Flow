⚠ Gulf Stream Wind-Against-Current Warning

ESE winds of 20-25 kt blowing against the northward-flowing Gulf Stream create steep, closely spaced waves that are disproportionately dangerous for small vessels. The Stream sits just 7 nm east of Port Everglades and 10 nm east of Lake Worth today, meaning offshore anglers can reach hazardous conditions quickly. Stay inside the Stream's west wall until the SCA expires.
