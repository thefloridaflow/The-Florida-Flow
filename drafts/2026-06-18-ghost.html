⚠ Lightning on the Water: Know When to Go

Scattered thunderstorms are possible every afternoon this week, and any cell can produce locally dangerous winds and seas with little warning. If you hear thunder or see anvil clouds building inland, return to port immediately. The NWS notes locally hazardous conditions are possible with any storm, even on otherwise flat days.
