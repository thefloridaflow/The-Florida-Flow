The Briefing

A front is on the way — enjoy the calm while it lasts

Conditions along the southeast Florida coast are comfortable this morning. Buoy 41122 (23 nm offshore of Deerfield through Miami) is reading 1.3 ft seas on a 3-second period, and the Treasure Coast buoy 41114 (6.5 nm off Vero/Fort Pierce) is at 1.6 ft on a 3-second period. Short-period chop at these heights means surface texture but the bottom is largely unaffected. South winds at 11 kt are being observed at LKWF1 (Blue Heron Bridge inshore), consistent with the NWS synopsis describing an approaching frontal boundary pulling winds south-southwesterly ahead of it.
