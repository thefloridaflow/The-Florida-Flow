The Briefing

Front on the Horizon, But Friday Is All Yours

A high-pressure ridge is holding conditions flat today, with seas at 1.0 ft along the Gold Coast and light westerly winds keeping visibility favorable. Enjoy it, because a frontal boundary is due to arrive Sunday with 74% precipitation chance and 25 mm of rain forecast.

Saturday is the transition day. Southerly winds will enhance across the northern Gulf Stream waters to SCEC thresholds, and NWS warns a brief Small Craft Advisory is possible if models are underdone. Plan offshore trips today, not this weekend.
