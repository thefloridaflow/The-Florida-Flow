The Briefing

Quiet seas, but the sky will not cooperate, all week long

Winds are light out of the SW at 5-10 kt and seas are sitting at 1 ft or less along the Gold Coast, with the Space Coast the roughest spot at 2.6 ft on a long 9-second swell. The NWS confirms gentle westerlies will prevail for the next several days, keeping wave heights low.

The catch is rain. Multiple rounds of showers and storms are expected from today through Wednesday, with Sunday at 81% precip chance topping the list. A northerly swell from high pressure building into the western Atlantic may also nudge seas up near the Gulf Stream early next week.
