Activity planner

ScubaCaution

Seas 3-4 ft with ESE winds 15-20 kt push most SE Florida sites into caution range. BHB wind at 18 kt exceeds the 12-15 kt no-go threshold; skip BHB today. Narcosis Dive reported 20-30 ft viz and great life mid-week, but today's wind is a different story.

SurfingCaution

Onshore ESE at 15-20 kt is blowing out the faces. Period is only 5s, limiting shape. Rideable but messy; Space Coast sees the most size at 4.6 ft.

Kayak / SUPCaution

Wind 18 kt and 3-4 ft seas exceed the good threshold. Stick to protected inlets, ICW, or mangrove waterways. Open ocean and exposed flats are not recommended today.

Boating / FishingCaution

No SCA in effect but NWS calls occasional 5 ft sets, which is uncomfortable for smaller vessels. Inshore and ICW fishing is a fine alternative. Offshore anglers should check updated forecasts before departure.

BeachCaution

UV index 9, Very High Alert today. Expect strong onshore ESE wind and a rough shorebreak. Yellow to red flag conditions likely along most of the SE Florida coast.

Gulf StreamCaution

No Gulf Stream hazards in effect per NWS. Stream wall sits 6-10 nm offshore from Fowey Rocks to Jupiter. Seas 3-5 ft make crossing uncomfortable; avoid with vessels under 25 ft today.
