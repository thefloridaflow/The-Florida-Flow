The Briefing

Issue #100: Calm by day, storm threat after sunset

South Florida woke to near-glassy offshore seas of 0.7 ft and light SW winds around 6 kt, with the NWS confirming seas under 2 ft across the Jupiter-to-Deerfield corridor. High pressure is holding, but scattered afternoon thunderstorms are expected to push offshore this evening with gusts modeled at 32 kt near 8 PM.

The window is real and wide this morning. Get your dive, paddle, or fishing run in before noon, and be dockside before the sea breeze and storm activity build in the late afternoon.
