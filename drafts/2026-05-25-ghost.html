A Breezy Memorial Day, and a Wetter Week Ahead

Southeast winds are running 13-19 kt today with gusts to 22 kt, pushing offshore seas to 4-6 ft along the Gold Coast and Space Coast. NWS has issued a Small Craft Advisory for Jupiter Inlet to Deerfield Beach, and caution is warranted across most of the southeast Florida coast.

The pattern turns unsettled midweek: drizzle arrives Wednesday, then rain showers dominate Thursday through Saturday with precip chances reaching 69% and accumulated rain totals of 18 mm or more by Thursday. Enjoy today's drier air before the week turns soggy.

Activity planner

ScubaCaution

Offshore seas at 4-4.6 ft with ESE winds 15-19 kt push conditions into caution. Narcosis Dive logged 70-80 ft viz on May 23 in milder conditions, but today's chop and ESE flow will reduce that. BHB wind is 19 kt, which is above the 12-15 kt no-go threshold for the bridge.

SurfingCaution

Seas 4-4.6 ft at 6s period offshore with onshore ESE winds 15-19 kt. Onshore wind at 15+ kt means blown-out conditions; rideable size but sloppy and choppy, not clean.

Kayak / SUPDo not go

Winds 13-19 kt and coastal chop well above the 2 ft / 10 kt threshold for safe paddling. Stick to calm inshore waterways only.

Boating / FishingCaution

Small Craft Advisory is active north of Deerfield Beach out to 20 nm. Offshore boating is not advised for smaller vessels. Inshore and inlet fishing remains a solid option today.

BeachCaution

UV index 9, Very High, with a UV Alert in effect. Yellow to red flag conditions along most of the coast due to ESE onshore winds 15-19 kt and 3-4 ft surf.

Gulf StreamDo not go

Seas at 4.3-4.6 ft offshore with an active SCA make Gulf Stream runs inadvisable today. The Stream wall sits 7-10 nm east of Port Everglades and Lake Worth, within the roughest water.
