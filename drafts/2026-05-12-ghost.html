The Briefing

A Frontal System Is Arriving Midweek, and Wednesday Looks Like the Day to Skip

Today brings rain showers with a 50% precipitation chance and gusts to 17kt at Palm Beach, conditions that are workable but worth watching. The real story is Wednesday, when thunderstorms arrive with 80% precip probability, gusts to 25kt, and NWS warning that northerly swell could push seas near advisory levels.

The front passes quickly, with Thursday through the weekend dropping back to drizzle conditions and winds under 15kt. Use today carefully, skip Wednesday offshore, and the back half of the week looks like a gradual return to normal South Florida summer patterns.
