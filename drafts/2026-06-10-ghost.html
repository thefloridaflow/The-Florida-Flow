⚠ Thunderstorm Plan Before You Launch

With afternoon storms flagged by the NWS today and precipitation chances climbing all week, have a hard turnaround time before heading offshore. Lightning over open water is the top marine safety risk in South Florida summers, and short-period 3-5 second seas can build quickly inside storm cells.
