A frontal boundary is sliding south across the Florida Peninsula today, driving breezy to gusty southerly winds that will trigger Small Craft Advisory conditions on northern Atlantic waters from noon to 8 PM, with Gulf Stream gusts reaching 25 kt.

The front arrives fully Sunday, bringing 83% rain chances and 9.1 mm of rain. Get any offshore plans done this morning or hold until Tuesday.
