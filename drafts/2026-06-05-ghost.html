The Briefing

A Fading NE Swell and a Wetter Week Ahead enjoy the drier days while they last

A northeasterly swell is slowly backing down along the South Florida coast today, with offshore seas at 3-6 ft and nearshore conditions running 2-4 ft, all trending lower through the weekend as winds shift more easterly.

The bigger story is next week: rain chances climb steadily from 13% Saturday to over 50% by Wednesday, and winds remain light, signaling a more humid, showery pattern taking hold through mid-June.

BeachCaution
