The Briefing

A Calm Window Opens but rain arrives by Sunday

South Florida waters are well-behaved this Wednesday, with buoys showing 1.3 ft seas off the Gold Coast and a gentle 6 kt ESE breeze at Palm Beach. Operator reports confirm the picture: calm waves, 50 ft viz at Snapper Ledge, and turtles returning off West Palm.

Enjoy it while it lasts. A pattern shift Sunday brings a 67% rain chance and 5.6 mm of rain, with Saturday already nudging up to 17 kt winds and 23 kt gusts. Wednesday through Friday is the sweet spot.

Activity planner

ScubaGood

Seas at or below 2 ft across the Gold Coast and Keys, winds under 10 kt. Rainbow Reef confirmed 50 ft viz at Snapper Ledge Tuesday. Note: dredging near West Palm cut second-dive viz to 20 ft per Narcosis Dive.

SurfingCaution

Space Coast buoy shows 3.3 ft at 8s, the best swell in the region today. Onshore SE wind limits quality but period is respectable. Gold Coast and Palm Beach too small for most boards.

Kayak / SUPGood

Winds at 6 kt ESE and seas under 2 ft inshore make this a green-light day. Conditions tick up tonight as gusts approach 16-18 kt, so morning is preferred.

Boating / FishingGood

No SCA in effect. Offshore seas 2-4 ft with occasional 5 ft per NWS, manageable for most vessels. Inshore and intracoastal conditions are light chop or calmer.

BeachGood

UV index 9 (Very High) today and tomorrow. Apply SPF 30 or higher and seek shade midday. Water temps near 80F are very comfortable for swimming.

Gulf StreamGood

Gulf Stream wall sits 3-7 nm offshore between Jupiter and Port Everglades, no hazards flagged. Seas 2-4 ft are within normal operating range; no adverse crossing conditions today.
