Activity planner

ScubaCaution

Offshore reefs: 2-3 ft ESE seas and 14 kt winds put conditions at the caution threshold. Narcosis Dive logged 60-70 ft viz on May 17 with similar conditions, so reefs can be productive if you can handle the chop on the surface.

SurfingCaution

2-3 ft ESE swell with 4-5 second periods is weak and wind-affected. Space Coast has the best shape at 3.6 ft with a 9-second period, but ESE onshore wind is not ideal. Not blown out, but not clean.

Kayak / SUPCaution

14 kt ESE winds push kayak and SUP conditions into caution territory. Stick to protected Intracoastal waters; open-water paddling is not recommended today.

Boating / FishingGood

No SCA in effect. Seas of 2-3 ft with 10-15 kt winds are manageable for most vessels. Thursday looks rougher with gusts to 22 kt, so prioritize Wednesday or weekend trips.

BeachGood

Partly cloudy skies and warm water in the low 80s make for a pleasant beach day. UV index hits 9 (Very High), so sun protection is essential.

Gulf StreamGood

No Gulf Stream hazards posted by NWS. West wall sits 7-11 nm offshore between Port Everglades and Jupiter. Seas are within range for experienced offshore anglers.
