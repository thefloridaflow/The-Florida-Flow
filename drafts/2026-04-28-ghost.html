The Briefing

NE Chop Holds Offshore Today, Window Opens Wednesday

A persistent NE flow is keeping offshore seas at 3-5 ft with an 8-second period today, confirmed by buoy 41009 at 4.6 ft. That combination keeps most offshore activity on hold until Wednesday, when SE winds at 5-10 kt are expected to flatten seas to 2-4 ft.

The week turns unsettled again by Saturday, with winds building to 17 kt and gusts to 23 kt. Sunday carries a 47% precip chance and 3 mm of rain, the most significant system in the 7-day window.
