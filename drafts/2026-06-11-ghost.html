⚠ Afternoon Thunderstorms: The Real Hazard This Week

With seas calm and winds light, the primary risk through this period is isolated afternoon thunderstorms, which can produce sudden waterspouts and dangerous lightning. NWS notes locally hazardous winds and seas are possible with any storm cell. Monitor radar before heading offshore and return to shore well before any storm builds.
