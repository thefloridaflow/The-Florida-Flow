High Pressure Keeps It Flat, But Watch the Evening Storm Window

High pressure dominates South Florida waters through the week, holding seas at 1 to 2 feet with light south winds, making this a solid stretch for most water activities in the morning hours.

The one caveat: slow-moving afternoon thunderstorms could drift offshore by evening, producing locally hazardous winds and seas, so plan your return well before sunset each day.
