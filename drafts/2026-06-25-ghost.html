⚠ Afternoon Storm Protocol

Pop-up thunderstorms are possible each afternoon this week, with Tuesday showing a 51% chance. Any cell can produce locally rough seas and dangerous lightning with little warning, so be off the water or in a safe harbor before storms develop. The rule is simple: if you hear thunder, you are already too late to start heading in.
