A persistent ESE flow at 12-15 kt is pushing 3.9-4.6 ft short-period seas along the Gold Coast and Space Coast today, with gusts touching 24 kt by afternoon. Conditions are choppy but not dangerous for boaters who stay close to shore.

The pattern softens slowly through the week, with winds dropping toward 10-11 kt by Friday and seas settling. No front is approaching and no named advisory is in effect.
