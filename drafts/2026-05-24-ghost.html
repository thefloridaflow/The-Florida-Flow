Activity planner

ScubaCaution

Seas at 3.9 ft with a short 5s period push most sites into caution territory. Narcosis reports 70-80 ft vis at West Palm Beach, but entry and exit on the boat can be rough in these conditions.

SurfingCaution

3.9 ft at 5s with onshore SE wind means crumbly, blown-out surf. Not ideal. Period is too short to produce clean faces.

Kayak / SUPDo not go

Winds at 12-15 knots SE with 3.9 ft seas put this well above the safe threshold for kayak and SUP. Stay inshore or on flat water.

Boating / FishingCaution

Small Craft Caution is in effect from Jupiter to Deerfield Beach out 20 nm. Inshore fishing is fine. Experienced captains only offshore today.

BeachCaution

Partly cloudy skies and 83 F water make for a pleasant beach day, but a yellow flag is warranted with 3.9 ft seas and onshore SE winds at 15 knots.

Gulf StreamCaution

No Gulf Stream hazards from NWS. Stream wall sits 7-10 nm east of Port Everglades and Lake Worth. Seas at 3.9 ft with short period make crossing uncomfortable but not dangerous for well-equipped vessels.
