⚠ Thunderstorm Protocol: Know Before You Go

With storms possible every afternoon this week, the 30-30 rule applies: if the gap between lightning and thunder is 30 seconds or less, get off the water immediately and wait 30 minutes after the last strike. Wednesday shows gusts to 27 kt with any cell, which can capsize kayaks and small boats with no warning.
