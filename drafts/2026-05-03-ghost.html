The Briefing

A Cold Front Is Clearing South Florida seas 3-5 ft offshore, SCEC possible through Monday

A frontal boundary is pressing southward today, driving NW winds 10-15 kt and building offshore seas to 3-5 ft, occasionally 6 ft between Jupiter Inlet and Deerfield Beach. Brief small-craft exercise caution windows are possible through Monday night on the northern Atlantic waters.

Winds ease and veer NE by midweek as the boundary stalls near the Florida Straits. South of Palm Beach, seas remain calmer at 1-2 ft, and the Keys are mostly unaffected.
