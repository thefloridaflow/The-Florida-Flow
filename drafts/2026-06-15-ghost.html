Flat Seas, Light SW Winds but Afternoon Storms Possible Daily

Conditions are calm to borderline-good across most of South Florida today, with seas at 0.3 ft offshore the Gold Coast and winds running 10 kt or less inshore. The Space Coast is the exception at 2.3 ft and 9s period, which is above average for that stretch.

The bigger story this week is a persistent scattered-storm pattern with precip chances every day through Saturday. Go early, watch radar, and be off the water before afternoon.
