The Briefing

Front Tracking South Wednesday, Advisory Seas Possible by Late Week Get out early today if you can

A front will push southward through Florida Wednesday through Thursday, increasing winds and driving a more significant NE swell across Atlantic waters. NWS notes advisory-level seas are possible by late week, so plan offshore trips carefully.

Today still has usable windows early, with W winds holding near 10 kt and nearshore seas in the 2-4 ft range, but scattered showers are already in the forecast and the afternoon brings the NE shift.
